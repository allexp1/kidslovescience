Common questions

How do I unlock a whole story?
The first three pages of every story are free. Tap “Ask a grown-up”, answer the grown-ups question, and buy the story once. It is yours on every device signed in with the same Apple Account, and with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought a story, but it is locked on a new device.
Open Grown-ups › Purchases › Restore Purchases.

Which languages are there?
Narration in English, Hebrew and Russian (The City of Gold in Hebrew). Story text and the app itself in 15 languages. Change them in Grown-ups › Language.

Can my child use it at bedtime?
Yes. Bedtime mode keeps the pictures still, turns off interface sounds and stops at the end of the story.

A refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
