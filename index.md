# Kids Love Science Stories

Grandpa Benny's illustrated science stories, read aloud. For iPhone and iPad, ages 6 to 8.

Grandpa Benny has been to the Moon, to the North Pole, even to the Wright brothers' bicycle shop. Children curl up with his grandchildren David and Maya as he tells the story of each adventure, and a science corner after every story explains the real facts behind it.

[Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823911169)

## Price

The app is free to download. Two stories are completely free: "Flies to the Moon" and "The Pirates of Galápagos". Each of the other six stories is a one-time purchase of 1.99 USD (or the local equivalent), shared with your family through Family Sharing. There is no subscription, no ads, no account and no tracking.

Narration is in English, Hebrew and Russian (The City of Gold is narrated in Hebrew), and each sentence lights up as it is read. Change them in Grown-ups › Language.

## Common questions

**How do I unlock a story?** Open the story, tap "Ask a grown-up", answer the grown-ups question, and buy the story once. It is yours on every device signed in with the same Apple Account, and with your family through Family Sharing.

**I bought a story, but it is locked on a new device.** Open Grown-ups › Purchases › Restore Purchases.

**Can my child use it at bedtime?** Yes. Bedtime mode keeps the pictures still, turns off interface sounds and stops at the end of the story.

**Is it safe for children?** Yes. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking, bookmarks stay on the device, and purchases and settings sit behind a grown-ups gate. See the [privacy policy](https://allexp1.github.io/kidslovescience/privacy.html).

**A refund?** Purchases are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
